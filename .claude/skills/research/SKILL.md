# Market Research

Read `CLAUDE.md`, the active session files through `01-vision.md`, `.claude/references/SESSION-AND-EVIDENCE.md`, and `.claude/references/MARKET-RESEARCH.md`.

## Entry gate

Run the mandatory progression gate in `CLAUDE.md`. Require `Path: Full`, `Next command: /research`, and complete approved `00-setup.md` and `01-vision.md`. Otherwise stop and name the earliest incomplete command.

## Scope

State the proposed research question, market/geography, and timebox in a compact paragraph. Ask one scope-confirmation question only if a material choice remains unresolved. Otherwise begin research.

Search the web when available. Open and inspect every source used. First look for category structure and customer alternatives, then investigate relevant companies, status-quo behaviors, offer patterns, pricing or commitment, proof, risks, and customer language.

Use the active session mode:

- **Workshop:** a focused pass sufficient to expose useful patterns and contradictions.
- **Deep Research:** broader source coverage, additional queries, and follow-up of contradictory leads.

Do not ask the participant to name all competitors. Their examples are leads, not the complete set.

## Analysis

Include:

- direct alternatives;
- indirect alternatives;
- status quo or doing nothing;
- crowded promises and recurring mechanisms;
- decision criteria and risk signals;
- useful customer language with provenance;
- gaps, contradictions, and limitations;
- potential differentiation ideas tested for relevance, distinction, proof, and deliverability.
- price, fulfillment, capacity, and operating-model signals that could constrain the offer.

Avoid unsupported market-size estimates and arbitrary scorecards.

## Synthesis checkpoint

Present at most five bullets: the proposed conclusions, the strongest disconfirming evidence, and the remaining uncertainty. Distinguish checked facts, inference, and hypothesis. Ask one confirmation or correction question.

## Save

After confirmation, write only `02-market-research.md`. Include:

1. scope and method;
2. source-backed alternative landscape;
3. meaningful comparison;
4. findings using the observation → interpretation → implication structure;
5. differentiation hypotheses;
6. evidence-strength table with `Safe to claim` and `Safe to test`;
7. commercial and operational signals for `/offer`;
8. source records and limitations;
9. decisions and open questions for `/audience`.

Update `SESSION.md`. Finish with the outcome, saved file, and “Next: `/audience`”.
