# Positioning

Read `CLAUDE.md`, active session files through `04-offer.md`, `.claude/references/SESSION-AND-EVIDENCE.md`, `.claude/references/MARKET-RESEARCH.md`, and `.claude/references/CONVERSION-STRATEGY.md`.

## Entry gate

Run the mandatory progression gate in `CLAUDE.md`. Require `Path: Full`, `Next command: /positioning`, and complete approved stage files through `04-offer.md`. Otherwise stop and name the earliest incomplete command.

## Positioning analysis

Use the approved alternative landscape. Do not reduce positioning to a tagline or adjective list.

Build the chain:

1. genuine alternatives in the priority situation;
2. capabilities or approach that differ;
3. value those differences create;
4. audience that cares most;
5. market frame that makes the value understandable;
6. proof available and proof still required.

Ask one question at a time only where an unresolved business choice changes the positioning.

Develop up to three positioning directions when evidence supports a real choice. Each direction must say what it emphasizes, which alternative it is compared with, who it fits, what must be proved, and what it deliberately gives up.

Re-run the relevant / distinct / provable / deliverable test. A direction that does not pass all four remains a positioning hypothesis. Participant approval selects it for testing but cannot make it a proven differentiator. Preserve this status in the saved file and downstream claim guardrails.

Finalize `D5` in the business decision spine from `04-offer.md`. Verify that the selected difference matters in the `D1` situation, improves on the `D2` alternative, follows from `D4`, respects `D7`, and creates a concrete customer-facing consequence. Preserve the other spine rows unchanged unless the participant explicitly revises them.

## Synthesis checkpoint

Recommend one direction and show:

- plain-language positioning decision;
- competitive frame;
- differentiated value;
- reason to believe;
- claims to avoid;
- falsifying evidence or test.

Ask one confirmation question.

## Save

After confirmation, write only `05-positioning.md`. Include alternative directions considered, decision rationale, the four-part differentiation test, status as evidence-backed decision or test hypothesis, risks, falsifying evidence, message guardrails, and the updated canonical `D1`–`D9` business decision spine. Update `SESSION.md`.

Finish with the outcome, saved file, and “Next: `/brand-concept`”.
