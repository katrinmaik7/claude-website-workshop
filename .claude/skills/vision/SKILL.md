# Vision

Read `CLAUDE.md`, the active session's `SESSION.md` and `00-setup.md`, plus `.claude/references/SESSION-AND-EVIDENCE.md`.

## Entry gate

Run the mandatory progression gate in `CLAUDE.md`. Require `Path: Full`, `Next command: /vision`, and a complete approved `00-setup.md`. Otherwise stop and direct the participant to `/setup`.

Do not browse yet. This stage records the participant's intent and assumptions so later research can test them.

## Questions

Ask one at a time, skipping only when the answer is already explicit:

1. If this offer works, what becomes different for the customer in practical terms?
2. What makes that change valuable now?
3. Why is this business able or motivated to help with it?
4. What must the website and offer never imply, promise, or pressure people to do?
5. What early signal would show that the website is attracting the right people?

Ask for a concrete example if an answer is abstract. Do not rewrite ambition as evidence.

## Synthesis checkpoint

Propose:

- intended customer change;
- business purpose;
- primary website action;
- 2–4 principles or boundaries;
- early success signals;
- assumptions that research must challenge.

Label all untested claims `Reported — founder` or `Hypothesis`. Ask one confirmation question.

## Save

After confirmation, write only `01-vision.md`. Include source labels, contradictions, open questions, and research questions for `/research`. Update `SESSION.md`.

Finish with what was decided, the saved file, and “Next: `/research`”.
