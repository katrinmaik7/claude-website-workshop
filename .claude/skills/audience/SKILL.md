# Audience Research

Read `CLAUDE.md`, active session files through `02-market-research.md`, `.claude/references/SESSION-AND-EVIDENCE.md`, and `.claude/references/AUDIENCE-RESEARCH.md`.

## Entry gate

Run the mandatory progression gate in `CLAUDE.md`. Require `Path: Full`, `Next command: /audience`, and complete approved stage files through `02-market-research.md`. Otherwise stop and name the earliest incomplete command.

## Founder input

When the supplied material does not already contain a concrete customer situation, ask one question: “Tell me about one real person or real situation that best represents why someone would look for this offer. What happened?”

If suitable interview, sales, support, or community evidence already contains that situation, use it and proceed without asking. If no real example exists, accept that and label the starting view as a hypothesis. Do not conduct a long founder interview before independent research.

## Independent evidence pass

Inventory relevant evidence in assets and earlier stages. Then search independently when web access is available for:

- triggering situations and current behavior;
- existing workarounds and alternatives;
- pains, tensions, and desired outcomes;
- objections, switching risks, trust needs, and decision criteria;
- exact public language with context and source.

Reuse relevant sources already checked in `02-market-research.md`. Do not repeat the same searches merely to create a separate audience file; search again only to answer an audience-specific gap or verify a material contradiction.

Public comments and reviews are directional evidence. Do not treat them as representative interviews or validated demand.

## Analysis

Build a situation-based audience view, not a decorative persona. Compare plausible segments and recommend one selected test audience for the first website version. Call it evidence-backed only when it meets the threshold in `AUDIENCE-RESEARCH.md`. Explain why it is the best current test, what remains assumption, and what evidence could overturn the choice.

Map strong insights to website messages, proof, offer details, objections, and calls to action.

Complete the audience-owned outcome-contract fields from `SESSION-AND-EVIDENCE.md`: `D1` priority customer and trigger, `D3` desired progress, and the audience evidence for `D2`, `D7`, and `D8`. Include the customer-facing consequence and preserve material contradictions.

## Synthesis checkpoint

Present at most five bullets:

- priority audience and triggering situation;
- current behavior and alternative;
- evidence strength and what is safe to claim;
- main tensions, language, barriers, and trust cues;
- weakest assumption and next direct validation needed.

Ask one confirmation or correction question.

## Save

After confirmation, write only `03-audience-research.md`. Include the evidence matrix, decision-strength table, participant view versus independent evidence, `Safe to claim` versus `Safe to test`, the audience-owned outcome-contract fields, source records, content implications, limitations, and a direct-validation plan when evidence is weak. Update `SESSION.md`.

Finish with the outcome, saved file, and “Next: `/offer`”.
